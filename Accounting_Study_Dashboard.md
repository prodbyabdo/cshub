# SECTION 1 — THE DECIPHER KEY
### Translating Exam Language into Double-Entry Logic

> **Master Rule:** Every transaction has TWO sides. Ask yourself: *What is being exchanged?* and *How is it being settled?*

---

## 1.1 Ownership & People Phrases

| Exam Phrase | What It Means | Double-Entry Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **"Proprietor"** | The owner of the business | Their investment = Capital (Cr); their withdrawals = Drawings (Dr) |
| **"The business"** | The entity itself (separate from the owner) | All transactions are from the business's perspective |
| **"Started in business with..."** | Owner introducing capital | Dr Bank/Cash — Cr Capital |
| **"Owner introduced further capital"** | More money put in by owner | Dr Bank/Cash — Cr Capital |
| **"Proprietor withdrew cash for private use"** | Owner taking money out | Dr Drawings — Cr Cash/Bank |
| **"Owner took goods for own use"** | Owner taking stock | Dr Drawings — Cr Purchases |
| **"Lent us £X by cash/cheque"** | Someone outside the firm gives a loan | Dr Cash/Bank — Cr Loan from [Name] |
| **"We borrowed £X from..."** | Same as above | Dr Cash/Bank — Cr Loan from [Name] |

---

## 1.2 Buying Phrases

| Exam Phrase | What It Means | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Bought goods on time from X"** | Credit purchase of goods for resale | Purchases | X (supplier account) |
| **"Bought goods on credit from X"** | Same as above | Purchases | X (supplier account) |
| **"Purchased goods from X"** | Same — check if cash or credit | Purchases | X or Cash/Bank |
| **"Bought goods for cash"** | Immediate cash payment for stock | Purchases | Cash |
| **"Bought goods by cheque"** | Immediate bank payment for stock | Purchases | Bank |
| **"Bought a van on time from X"** | Credit purchase of a NON-CURRENT ASSET | Van | X (supplier account) |
| **"Bought machinery paying by cheque"** | Cash purchase of asset — never "Purchases" account | Machinery | Bank |
| **"Bought shop fixtures on time from X"** | Credit purchase of fixture — not Purchases account | Shop Fixtures | X (supplier account) |
| **"Bought stationery by cheque"** | Expense, not goods for resale | Stationery | Bank |

> ⚠️ **Critical Rule:** "Purchases" ONLY means goods bought for resale. A van, computer, or fixture is NEVER entered in the Purchases account — it gets its own asset account.

---

## 1.3 Selling Phrases

| Exam Phrase | What It Means | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Sold goods on time to X"** | Credit sale of goods | X (customer account) | Sales |
| **"Cash sales"** | Immediate cash received for goods | Cash | Sales |
| **"Sold goods by cheque"** | Immediate bank receipt for goods | Bank | Sales |
| **"Sold van for cash"** | Disposing of a NON-CURRENT ASSET — never "Sales" account | Cash | Van |

> ⚠️ **Critical Rule:** "Sales" ONLY refers to goods the business normally deals in. Selling a van or computer is credited to that asset's own account, not Sales.

---

## 1.4 Payments & Receipts

| Exam Phrase | What It Means | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Paid X by cheque"** | Business pays a creditor via bank | X (creditor account) | Bank |
| **"Paid X in cash"** | Business pays using cash | X (creditor account) | Cash |
| **"X paid us by cheque"** | Debtor settles their account via bank | Bank | X (debtor account) |
| **"X paid us in cash"** | Debtor settles in cash | Cash | X (debtor account) |
| **"Received cheque from X"** | Same as debtor paying by cheque | Bank | X (debtor account) |
| **"We settled our account with X"** | Business pays full amount owed | X (creditor) | Bank/Cash |

---

## 1.5 Returns Phrases

| Exam Phrase | What It Means | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"We returned goods to X"** | Business returns to supplier | X (supplier account) | Returns Outwards |
| **"Returned goods to supplier X"** | Same | X (supplier account) | Returns Outwards |
| **"X returned goods to us"** | Customer returns to business | Returns Inwards | X (customer account) |
| **"Goods returned by X"** | Same | Returns Inwards | X (customer account) |

> 🧠 **Memory Trick:** Returns OUT → goods going OUT to supplier → Credits reduce our liability. Returns IN → goods coming IN from customer → Debits increase our "cost."

---

## 1.6 Expense Phrases

| Exam Phrase | Account Name | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Paid rent by cash/cheque"** | Rent | Rent | Cash/Bank |
| **"Paid wages in cash"** | Wages | Wages | Cash |
| **"Paid business rates by cheque"** | Business Rates | Business Rates | Bank |
| **"Paid electricity by cheque"** | Electricity | Electricity | Bank |
| **"Paid carriage in cash"** | Carriage | Carriage | Cash |
| **"Paid motor expenses"** | Motor Expenses | Motor Expenses | Cash/Bank |
| **"Paid insurance"** | Insurance | Insurance | Cash/Bank |
| **"Paid repairs"** | Repairs | Repairs | Cash/Bank |

# SECTION 2 — MCQ CHEATSHEET
### Essential Definitions, Rules & Quick-Fire Facts

---

## 2.1 The Master Equation

```
ASSETS = CAPITAL + LIABILITIES
```

- **Assets** = what the business OWNS
- **Capital** = what the OWNER invested (also = Assets − Liabilities)
- **Liabilities** = what the business OWES to outsiders

---

## 2.2 The Two Axioms (Never Fail)

| Axiom | Rule | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| **Axiom 1** | Any INCREASE in a Possession (Asset or Expense) | → Always **DEBIT** |
| **Axiom 2** | Any INCREASE in Capital or Liability | → Always **CREDIT** |
| **Reverse** | The OPPOSITE circumstance | → Opposite entry |

---

## 2.3 Asset Classification

### Non-Current Assets (Long-term, NOT for resale)
- Land & Buildings, Motor Vans, Machinery, Equipment, Furniture, Fixtures, Computers

### Current Assets (Short-term, change within 12 months)
- Inventory (Stock), Accounts Receivable (Debtors), Cash, Bank Balance, Prepaid Expenses

---

## 2.4 Liability Classification

### Current Liabilities (Due within 1 year)
- Accounts Payable (Creditors), Bank Overdraft, Short-term loans

### Non-Current Liabilities (Due after 1 year)
- Long-term bank loans, Mortgages

---

## 2.5 The Four Inventory Accounts

| Account | Side | When Used |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchases** | Dr | Buying goods FOR RESALE |
| **Sales** | Cr | Selling goods the business normally deals in |
| **Returns Inwards** (Sales Returns) | Dr | Customer returns goods TO us |
| **Returns Outwards** (Purchases Returns) | Cr | We return goods TO supplier |

---

## 2.6 Trial Balance Quick Rules

| Always on DEBIT side | Always on CREDIT side |
|---|---|
| Assets | Liabilities |
| Expenses | Capital |
| Drawings | Revenues (Sales) |
| Returns Inwards | Returns Outwards |
| Losses | Profits |

**Purpose:** Proves Total Debits = Total Credits  
**Limitation:** Does NOT catch errors of omission, commission, or compensating errors

---

## 2.7 Balance c/d and b/d Logic

```
Step 1: Total both sides (mentally)
Step 2: Find the difference = the balance
Step 3: Write Balance c/d on the SMALLER side (to make both sides equal)
Step 4: Rule off with totals level on both sides
Step 5: Write Balance b/d on the OPPOSITE side, dated 1st of next month
```

| Account Type | Balance Type | Where is b/d? |
|---|---|---|
| Assets (Cash, Van, Debtors) | **Debit** balance | Left (Dr) side |
| Expenses (Rent, Wages) | **Debit** balance | Left (Dr) side |
| Liabilities (Loans, Creditors) | **Credit** balance | Right (Cr) side |
| Capital | **Credit** balance | Right (Cr) side |
| Revenue (Sales) | **Credit** balance | Right (Cr) side |

---

## 2.8 Key Definitions Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Accounting** | Identifying, measuring, and communicating economic information for decision-making |
| **Bookkeeping** | The recording part of accounting |
| **Debit** | Entry on the LEFT side of an account |
| **Credit** | Entry on the RIGHT side of an account |
| **Drawings** | Resources taken by the owner for private use |
| **Carriage Inwards** | Transport costs on goods PURCHASED (Dr — adds to cost of purchases) |
| **Carriage Outwards** | Transport costs on goods SOLD to customers (Dr — an expense) |
| **Gross Profit** | Sales Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold |
| **Net Profit** | Gross Profit − All other expenses |
| **"On Time"** | Credit transaction — no immediate cash exchange |
| **Bank Overdraft** | Current Liability (bank owes you nothing; you owe the bank) |
